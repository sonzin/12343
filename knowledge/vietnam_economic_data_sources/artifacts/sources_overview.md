# Vietnam Economic and Financial Data Sources - Overview

This document lists the primary official and international sources for tracking and analyzing the Vietnamese economy.

## 🏛️ Official Domestic Sources

### 1. General Statistics Office (GSO / NSO)
The primary authority for socio-economic statistics in Vietnam.
- **Website:** [nso.gov.vn](https://www.nso.gov.vn)
- **Key Data:**
    - **GDP:** Economic growth figures (Annual, Quarterly).
    - **CPI:** Consumer Price Index and inflation rates.
    - **Industrial Production:** IPI index.
    - **Trade:** Import/Export statistics.
    - **Labor:** Unemployment rates and labor force data.

### 2. State Bank of Vietnam (SBV)
The central bank of Vietnam.
- **Website:** [sbv.gov.vn](https://www.sbv.gov.vn)
- **Key Data:**
    - **Interest Rates:** Policy rates, refinancing rates, and base rates.
    - **Exchange Rates:** Daily central exchange rate between VND and USD.
    - **Monetary Policy:** Reports and decisions on credit growth and banking regulations.

---

## 🌍 International Databases

### 1. World Bank
Provides extensive historical time-series data.
- **Data Portal:** [data.worldbank.org/country/vietnam](https://data.worldbank.org/country/vietnam)
- **Indicators:** GDP, FDI, Inflation, Population, Poverty Headcount.

### 2. International Monetary Fund (IMF)
Focuses on macroeconomic stability and financial sector assessments.
- **Data Portal:** [data.imf.org](https://data.imf.org)

### 3. Trading Economics
Aggregates data from multiple sources and provides historical charts and forecasts.
- **Website:** [tradingeconomics.com/vietnam](https://tradingeconomics.com/vietnam)

## 📈 Financial News & Market Data Aggregators

These sites are commonly used for real-time market sentiment and financial news in Vietnam.
- **CafeF:** [cafef.vn](https://cafef.vn) (Stock market, real estate, finance)
- **Vietstock:** [vietstock.vn](https://vietstock.vn) (Detailed stock and financial data)
- **VNExpress Finance:** [vnexpress.net/kinh-doanh/tai-chinh-tui-tien](https://vnexpress.net/kinh-doanh/tai-chinh-tui-tien)
